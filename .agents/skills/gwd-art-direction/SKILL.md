# GWD art direction

1. Read docs/ART_DIRECTION.md and docs/MOTION_DIRECTION.md.
2. Start from hierarchy, audience and content. Never decorate before the information architecture is understood.
3. Use the approved dark navy palette. Reserve cyan/blue for meaningful signals; use warm metallic accents rarely.
4. Design for calm depth, precise geometry, negative space and readable typography.
5. Ensure the organization reads before interaction begins.
6. Keep five organizational strata distinct and the Agency Agents field visually separate from reporting hierarchy.
7. Use animation to reveal or explain. Avoid random movement and perpetual effects.
8. Present proposed/illustrative/catalogued/active states in explicit text, not colour alone.
9. Check desktop, tablet and mobile composition.
10. Provide a review report with screenshots, strengths, defects and acceptance criteria.
