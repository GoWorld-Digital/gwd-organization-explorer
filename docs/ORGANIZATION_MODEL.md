# Proposed GWD organizational model

## Status and interpretation
This document defines a proposed model for the Explorer. It is not a corporate registry, legal opinion, board appointment record or claim that all functions currently exist. All nodes should carry an explicit status. Default status is proposed.

## Layer 1 — Governance
### Board of Administration
Proposed remit: purpose and long-term direction; capital allocation and strategic commitments; risk appetite and legal oversight; executive appointment and performance; organizational health.

### Advisory and Strategic Council
A flexible advisory body, not automatically a statutory board. It may include domain experts, researchers, partners, investors or independent advisors. Participation does not imply employment, equity or board appointment.

## Layer 2 — Executive leadership
### CEO and Executive Direction
Translates the mandate into strategy, priorities, resource allocation and performance expectations.

### Executive Coordination / AI Operations
Coordinates missions, dependencies, evidence, operating status and approvals. The future GWD Ops Core may support this function, but the Explorer must not pretend it is already the live operating system.

Human sign-off is mandatory for legal commitments, investments, customers, payments, spending, material risk acceptance and other critical actions.

## Layer 3 — Transversal shared services
1. Strategy, Research & Intelligence
2. Finance, Administration & Performance
3. People, Talent & Agent Operations
4. AI Platform, Engineering & Automation
5. Legal, Risk, Compliance & Governance
6. Marketing, Brand & Communications
7. Product, Design & Experience
8. Security, Data Governance & Quality

These capabilities serve multiple business units. They may initially be central cost centres, later gaining service-level agreements, chargeback or internal budgets.

## Layer 4 — Business units and economic responsibility
### GWD Academy
Education, training, learning experiences, communities, certification and knowledge products.

### GWD Media Factory & Growth
Content production, brand media, campaigns, distribution, growth strategy and measurement.

### GWD Sales & Customer Operations
Sales operations, customer acquisition, CRM, support, service coordination and customer success.

### GWD Ventures & Venture Studio
Opportunity development, venture design, pilots, spin-outs, partnerships and potential portfolio participation. Ventures remain hypotheses until individually validated.

Business units are candidate profit centres when they own a defined offer, revenue, delivery costs and measurable contribution margin. Do not assert accounting status before management accounting exists.

## Layer 5 — Labs and innovation
- AI & Systems Lab: agentic systems, orchestration, memory, evaluation and applied AI.
- Organization Design Lab: AI-native operating models, human-agent collaboration and decision rights.
- Venture Lab: opportunity discovery, problem validation, prototypes and spin-off preparation.

Labs are initially investment/cost centres or time-bounded programs.

## Economic model vocabulary
- Cost centre: owns a budget and provides capabilities; measured on cost, quality, utilization and efficiency.
- Profit centre: owns an offer and revenue responsibility; measured on revenue, direct costs, contribution margin, retention and acquisition efficiency.
- Investment centre: allocates capital to initiatives or ventures; measured on invested capital, milestones, risk and realized return over an appropriate horizon.
- Innovation lab: time-bounded learning and experimentation unit; measured on validated hypotheses, prototypes, learning velocity and transition outcomes.

## Reporting versus capability relationships
A reporting edge describes accountability. A service edge describes shared capability delivery. A capability edge maps a profile/domain to potential work. A mission edge identifies participants in a scenario. Visual styles must distinguish these relationship types.
