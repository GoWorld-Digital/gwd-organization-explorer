# Proposed GWD Organizational Model

**Persistent public label:** Proposed organizational model — subject to governance and legal formalization.

## Layer 1 — Governance
- Board of Administration (proposed): strategic direction, oversight, capital principles, risk and accountability.
- Advisory / Strategic Council (proposed): independent experts, researchers, partners and advisors; advisory rather than executive authority unless separately mandated.

## Layer 2 — Executive Leadership
- CEO & Executive Direction (proposed): strategy, priorities, portfolio performance and executive accountability.
- AI Operations / Ops Core (design principle): intended coordination layer for missions, approvals, budgets, evidence and reporting. This Explorer is not Ops Core and must not present it as operational.

## Layer 3 — Shared Services / transversal departments
- Strategy & Intelligence.
- Finance & Administration.
- People & Agent Operations.
- AI Platform & Engineering.
- Legal, Risk & Compliance.
- Marketing & Communications.
- Product & Innovation.
- Security & Data Governance.

These may begin as central cost centres; do not invent named office holders or claim the functions are staffed.

## Layer 4 — Business Units
- GWD Academy — education, training, learning communities, certification.
- GWD Media Factory & Growth — content, production, campaigns and growth.
- GWD Sales & Customer Operations — sales operations, CRM, service and customer success.
- GWD Ventures & Venture Studio — opportunity discovery, validation, incubation, spin-offs and partnerships.

Academy, Media and Sales are profit-centre candidates only once real products, revenue, cost allocation and accountability exist. Ventures is an investment-centre candidate, not an established fund.

## Layer 5 — Labs & Innovation
Potential labs: AI Research & Systems; Agentic Architecture; Data & Knowledge; Human-AI Interaction; Venture Experimentation. Labs are proposed/time-boxed experiments; promote to BU or venture only by explicit decision.

## Agency Agents as cross-cutting capability ecosystem
The upstream Agency Agents library is a taxonomy, not a reporting hierarchy. A capability may support several BUs. An agent profile is not an employee, contractor or active production agent.

## Relationship rules
- Governance provides oversight; executive direction coordinates.
- Shared services support several BUs with transparent cost allocation.
- BUs own customer/product outcomes.
- Labs explore uncertainty.
- Human approval remains necessary for legal, financial, client, security-sensitive and irreversible actions.
