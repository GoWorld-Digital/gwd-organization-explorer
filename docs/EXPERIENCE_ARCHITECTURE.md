# Experience architecture and information architecture

## Primary navigation
Overview · Organization · Capabilities · Business model · Mission simulator · Ventures & Labs · Methodology

## Route proposal
- / — cinematic overview and guided entry.
- /organization — five-layer organizational explorer.
- /capabilities — Agency Agents division and profile explorer.
- /business-model — cost, profit and investment-centre map.
- /missions — illustrative mission simulator.
- /ventures — labs, ventures and opportunity portfolio.
- /methodology — provenance, status definitions and limitations.

Routes may initially be implemented as sections/tabs if that creates a more coherent spatial experience.

## Landing sequence
1. Signal: dark, quiet opening; a precise field of points/lines resolves into a central form.
2. Thesis: “An organization designed around intelligence, capability and accountable execution.”
3. Reveal: five organizational layers separate and become readable.
4. Proof of structure: highlight business units, shared services and labs.
5. Capability universe: transition to 18 divisions and the curated profile catalogue.
6. Invitation: “Explore the organization” / “Simulate a mission”.

The visitor can skip the cinematic sequence immediately.

## Organization explorer
Desktop: central spatial view; left layer index; right information panel; persistent orientation/breadcrumb; 2D/3D switch; zoom-to-fit and reset.

Mobile: 2D-first, simplified optional 3D; bottom sheet or full-screen details; large touch targets; no hover-only instructions.

## Agency Agents explorer
Start at 18-division level with counts. Search profiles after source import. Filters: division, skill domain, review status and maturity. Profile view includes source identity, summary, division, source path/commit, license review, GWD mapping and status. Distinguish “source profile” from “GWD-adapted capability”.

## Mission simulator
Each scenario shows objective, assumptions, participating units, shared-service dependencies, candidate capabilities, human approval gates, illustrative steps and deliverables. First scenario: “Launch a digital learning product in two markets”. This is a declarative simulation, not live execution.

## Interaction grammar
- Select: reveal details and synchronize 2D/3D.
- Focus: frame selected node, unit or capability.
- Trace: highlight related services/capabilities.
- Compare: show alternative organizational designs.
- Simulate: animate a scenario through participating units.
- Reset: return to overview without losing context.
- All interactions have a textual equivalent and keyboard route.
