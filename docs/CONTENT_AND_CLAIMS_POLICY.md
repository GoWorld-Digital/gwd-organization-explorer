# Content, claims and visual representation policy

## Purpose
Build market authority through clarity and ambition, not by implying maturity, scale or performance that has not been evidenced.

## Required vocabulary
- Proposed: organizational design under exploration.
- Source catalogue: external Agency Agents profiles.
- Capability domain: one of the 18 divisions.
- Candidate capability: source profile mapped to possible GWD work.
- Tested: a defined test with evidence has passed.
- Active: operating in a GWD environment with verifiable evidence.
- Illustrative simulation: every mission and economic scenario in public release 1.

## Avoid
- “283 active agents”.
- “Fully autonomous company” as an operational claim.
- “Live organization” unless data is genuinely live and current.
- Unqualified revenue, customer, productivity, ROI or return claims.
- Fake testimonials, invented partners or fictional officeholders presented as real.

## Status indicators
Each organization node, capability or venture has an explicit status. Status is never represented by colour alone.

## Financial communication
Do not invent revenue, salaries, headcount, savings or margins. Scenario assumptions must state “Illustrative only”, currency and period, basis/formula, whether user inputs or samples, and that outputs are not forecasts.

## People and governance
Do not invent named officeholders. Use role names unless a person and their public role are confirmed and approved. Advisory community participation does not automatically imply employment, director status or equity.

## Sources
The Methodology route explains what comes from Agency Agents, what is GWD's proposed model, what is illustrative and what is independently verified as operational.
