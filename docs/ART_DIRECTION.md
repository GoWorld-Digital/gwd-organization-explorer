# Art direction — cinematic institutional technology

## Creative concept
**A living architecture of intelligence.**

The visual system should feel like an architectural observatory or a premium institutional control room: precise geometry, layered depth, restrained light, calm typography and meaningful motion.

Avoid crypto dashboards, generic cyberpunk, spaceship HUDs, animated-org-chart templates, random particles and excessive neon.

## Brand character
Strategic and authoritative; technologically advanced but comprehensible; international and institutional; curious and exploratory; confident without exaggerated claims.

## Palette proposal
- Void: #050913
- Deep navy: #081426
- Panel: #0D1B2B
- Elevated panel: #14243A
- Primary text: #F2F6FA
- Muted text: #9AABC0
- Cyan signal: #66D9E8
- Electric blue: #5C86FF
- Soft violet: #9C8CFF
- Warm metallic accent: #C9A96E (rare use for governance/capital)
- Green: #5BC89A (verified/operational only)
- Amber: #E7B86A (proposed/review)
- Red: #EF7373 (risk/blocked)

The interface remains predominantly dark navy. Accent colour encodes meaning rather than decoration.

## Typography
Geist Sans or a similarly neutral grotesk for UI and headings; Geist Mono for data and coordinates. Avoid very thin display fonts for essential content. Maintain readable body sizes and strong contrast.

## Spatial language
Five architectural strata, slightly separated vertically. Governance is a compact crown; executive leadership is the coordination core; shared services form a connective belt; business units are substantial modules; labs and ventures form an experimental/future zone. The Agency Agents universe orbits the organization as a capability field, not as a sixth reporting layer.

## Lighting and materials
Matte dark surfaces, thin emissive edges, selective translucent information overlays, one key light and soft rim light. Use bloom/volumetric effects sparingly; never bloom over text.

## Camera
Start at an oblique, readable three-quarter view. Ease toward orthographic-like framing for hierarchy/data inspection. Use damped movement, explicit focus states and reset. Never use uncontrolled free-flight camera behaviour.

## UI surfaces
Readable DOM panels, clear divider hierarchy and consistent spacing. Essential labels must remain in semantic DOM, not only 3D sprites.

## References
Architectural visualization, scientific observatories, premium enterprise data visualization, museum exhibition design and high-end product storytelling are quality references, not templates to copy.
