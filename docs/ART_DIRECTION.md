# Art Direction — Cinematic, Institutional, Precise

## Creative premise
**An executive observatory into an intelligent organization.** Combine architectural visualization, scientific instruments and premium editorial design. Communicate calm intelligence, scale, clarity and control.

Avoid generic purple AI gradients, cyberpunk tropes, random particle storms, game HUDs, excessive glassmorphism, avatar crowds or neon on every edge.

## Palette
- Abyss #07111F; deep navy #0B1930; elevated surface #11243A.
- Main text #EAF3F8; secondary #A8BBCB; quiet line #263B52.
- Intelligence cyan #67E8D2; cobalt #6D9DFF; violet #B59AFF; investment accent #E6B978; warning #E5A56D.

Use accents semantically, not as decoration. Typography: refined grotesk (Geist Sans or equivalent), readable body scale, tabular numerals. Prefer local/self-hosted fonts.

## Material vocabulary
Thin architectural plates, precise connectors, modest translucency, matte dark metal, soft directional and rim lighting, restrained haze. Layers must remain visually distinct. No blur over readable labels.

## Layout
Desktop: 12-column responsive grid, central stage, narrow navigation, collapsible contextual panel, provenance/status strip.
Tablet: simplified scene and touch-friendly controls.
Mobile: 2D-first; optional lightweight 3D preview; no hover-only labels.

## Motion
Movement should assemble, reveal, focus, connect and settle. Use controlled ease-out. No constant spinning, shaking, forced camera movement or scroll-jacking. Provide skip/reset/pause options.

## Sound
No autoplay. V1 works silently. Any later ambient sound must be opt-in, muted by default and controllable.
