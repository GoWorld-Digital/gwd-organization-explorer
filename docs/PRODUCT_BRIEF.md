# Product brief — GWD AI-Native Organization Explorer

## Product thesis
GWD should be understandable as an AI-Native organization, not presented as a traditional company chart with AI labels pasted onto it.

The Explorer makes the organizational design legible: governance establishes direction and accountability; executive leadership coordinates the organization; shared services provide common capabilities; business units create customer and economic value; labs and ventures test future opportunities; and an Agency Agents capability ecosystem can support work across multiple layers.

The experience is a public-facing explanation and interactive design/simulation surface. It is not proof that every proposed structure is already legally constituted or operational.

## Primary audiences
1. Business leaders and founders — understand how an AI-Native organization can be designed.
2. Potential clients — explore the service and capability architecture and imagine a practical use case.
3. Strategic partners — identify complementary capabilities, venture opportunities and collaboration points.
4. Investors and innovation stakeholders — understand the architecture and investment logic without misleading financial claims.
5. Researchers and builders — inspect the model, capability taxonomy and governance principles.

## User promise
“In a few minutes, understand how an AI-Native organization is structured, what capabilities it can draw on, and how those capabilities can be assembled around a real business mission.”

## Experience outcomes
Visitors should be able to understand the five layers; open units and functions; distinguish governance, coordination, shared services, operations and experimentation; browse 18 source divisions and curated profiles; understand cost, profit and investment centres; run an illustrative mission; see what is proposed versus evidenced; and share an organizational view.

## Product modes
### Explore GWD
A guided cinematic public experience. This is the default entry point and strongest marketing surface.

### Design an AI-Native Organization
A sandbox mode. Visitors can add/remove proposed nodes, allocate illustrative capabilities and test how a mission might be staffed. Changes are local to the session and are not saved to GWD systems in release 1.

### Future: Live organization view
A separately governed future capability that may display selected operational information from GWD Ops Core once that system exists, is authorized and has stable APIs. Explicitly out of scope for release 1.

## Product principles
- Clarity before spectacle.
- Serious and cinematic: premium motion without a game-like interface.
- Evidence-aware: provenance and maturity labels are visible.
- Capability over hierarchy: profiles may be cross-functional.
- Human accountability for critical decisions.
- Progressive disclosure: show the full organization first, details on selection.
- Public by design: no user account required for release 1.
