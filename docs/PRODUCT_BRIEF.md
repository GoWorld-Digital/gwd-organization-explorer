# Product Brief — GWD AI-Native Organization Explorer

**Status:** blueprint, not implemented. **Proposed public URL:** explore.goworld.digital. **Snapshot date:** 8 Nov 2026.

## Vision
A premium, cinematic and evidence-aware digital experience that makes the proposed GWD AI-Native organization understandable. Visitors explore governance, executive direction, shared services, business units, Labs, the Agency Agents capability library and illustrative missions.

The feeling should be an architectural observatory / executive command environment, not a generic dashboard or sci-fi game.

## Objectives
1. Demonstrate strategic and technological authority.
2. Explain how an AI-Native organization may be governed and operated.
3. Make the proposed model explorable without claiming it is fully constituted.
4. Expose the breadth of Agency Agents source capabilities and potential applications.
5. Explain how a hypothetical mission mobilizes capabilities, units and human approval gates.
6. Leave a future path to approved read-only Ops Core data, without depending on it now.

## Audiences
Executives, business owners, prospective clients, partners, researchers, advisors, potential investors and GWD contributors.

## V1 boundaries
Public, read-only, curated, versioned static data. No login, personal-data collection, live agent execution, financial actuals, database, Supabase, Hermes, Ops Core or CRM connection. Do not include private company data.

## Five experiences
1. Organization Explorer.
2. Agency Agents Explorer.
3. Business Architecture Simulator.
4. Mission Simulator.
5. Ventures & Innovation.

## Success measures
- A first-time visitor understands the five layers in under two minutes.
- Every unit has a status: proposed, catalogued, evaluated, adapted, tested or operational.
- 18 divisions / 283 source profiles reconcile with the recorded source.
- The product works without 3D, with reduced motion and on mobile.
- No unsupported organizational or financial claims.

## Positioning
**Explore the architecture of an AI-Native organization.** A living map of governance, intelligent capabilities and business value.

This is a proposed model and exploration environment, not proof every unit or agent is operational.
