## Notes on model/tool selection
- ChatGPT Work is suited to longer multi-step research and coordination; Codex is the dedicated software-development environment.
- GPT-6 Astra is available in eligible Work/Codex surfaces; exact access and usage depend on account, plan and rollout.
- Claude Opus 5.5 is positioned for long-running agentic coding and complex knowledge work.
- Antigravity currently lists Gemini 4 Argon and Claude Opus 5.5 options on eligible paid plans. Model access is account/plan dependent.
- The model name alone does not guarantee a premium result. Browser iteration, screenshots, tests and evidence-based review are mandatory.
